# Netter-labeled structures without a card

The 2026-11-01 review listed 112 Netter 7e structures with no card (35 arteries, 33 bony landmarks, 22 veins, 16 nerves, 4 muscles, 2 node groups). On 2026-11-04 cards were written for the ones that matter at Step 2 CK depth (33 bone, 3 muscle, 12 nerve, 2 lymph, 33 artery, 15 vein = 98 cards, every fact taken from the Netter plates cited in the commit). Only the items below were left out, each on purpose.

## bone (3 skipped)

- Trigeminal impression, arcuate eminence, carotid groove, prechiasmatic groove (Plate 18) — not given a separate card: the arcuate eminence is covered in the new bony-labyrinth card, the carotid groove and prechiasmatic groove in the sella turcica and cranial fossae cards, the trigeminal impression in the trigeminal ganglion card. A card of four unrelated grooves would not be examinable.
- Jugular process of the occipital bone (Plate 37) — only an attachment for rectus capitis lateralis; mentioned inside the new occipital-condyles card.
- Cervical intervertebral discs C2–C3 to C7–T1 and their nuclei pulposi (model labels, Plate 28) — not in the original Netter list; left unmatched because disc herniation is taught with the spine, not head and neck.

## muscle (1 skipped)

- Petropharyngeus (accessory muscle bundle from the petrous temporal bone, Plate 75) — an inconstant slip with no Netter muscle-table row and no clinical relevance.

## nerve (4 skipped)

- Pharyngeal branch of the pterygopalatine ganglion (Plate 46, BP25) — a single sensory twig to the nasopharynx; already named in the pterygopalatine ganglion card.
- Jugular nerve of the superior cervical ganglion (Plate 141) — a communicating twig to the IX and X ganglia; the superior cervical ganglion card covers the ganglion's communications.
- Vertebral ganglion and vertebral plexus (Plate 141) — variant ganglion on the vertebral artery; covered by the cervical sympathetic trunk and stellate ganglion cards.
- Posterior cutaneous branches of the posterior rami C4–C7 (Plates 9, 183–184) — trivial cutaneous twigs of the posterior neck; the greater, third occipital and suboccipital nerve cards cover the testable posterior rami.

## artery (2 skipped)

- Inferior tympanic artery (Plates 64, 107) — small branch of the ascending pharyngeal artery to the middle ear; already listed in the ascending pharyngeal card, and the tympanic plexus/glomus tympanicum pearl sits on the new tympanic-and-mastoid-canaliculi card.
- Middle temporal, zygomaticoorbital and anterior auricular arteries (Plate 10) — minor superficial temporal branches with no examinable point beyond the superficial temporal artery card.

## vein (7 skipped)

- Inferior cerebral veins (Plates 113, 115, 156) — unnamed cortical veins of the temporal lobe base; covered by the new anastomotic-veins (Labbé) and superficial middle cerebral vein cards.
- Petrosal vein (Plates 115, 155) — described inside the superior petrosal sinus card.
- Transverse facial vein and middle temporal vein (Plate 10) — small superficial temporal tributaries, listed in the superficial temporal vein card.
- Communicating vein between the facial/retromandibular veins and the anterior jugular vein (Plates 38, 84) — a variable connection, named in the anterior jugular vein card.
- Infraorbital, external nasal, superior and inferior labial, mental, external palatine, palatine, posterior superior alveolar and inferior alveolar veins (Plates 10, 84) — each is a one-line tributary; they are listed by name in the facial vein and pterygoid venous plexus cards.
- Sublingual, deep lingual and dorsal lingual veins and the vena comitans of the hypoglossal nerve (Plates 70, 84) — listed in the lingual vein card.

## Reviewer notes from the 2026-11-01 review (kept for reference)

The earlier per-category notes recorded where Netter is silent or uses different names; the key points still apply to the new cards: Netter's plates give labels only, so courses, relations and all clinical statements in the new cards are standard anatomy consistent with (but not stated by) the labels; Netter assigns the emissary vein and meningeal branch of the ascending pharyngeal artery to the condylar canal; Netter uses 'External (dorsal) nasal artery', 'Greater (descending) palatine artery', 'Labyrinthine (internal acoustic) artery', 'posterior ramus', 'vestibular (oval) window' and 'lateral process of septal nasal cartilage', and these spellings were used in the new card names where they apply.
